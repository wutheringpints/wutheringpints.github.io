Slowly getting further and further away from any "civil" area of Hongyuan, Yang grew more and more on-edge. He knew he could take any group of rats that would come his way by himself, so more than anything he was worried for Yolisa getting separated by some stroke of misfortune.

He thought back to the question he asked her before, and how she seemed to have dodged answering as to why she hadn’t gone back yet. Sure, it might draw attention to her if Yaksha was still in the area, but that would also bring them much closer to their goal. He felt that if one of them were to try fighting her on their own, it wouldn’t end nearly as well.

Another thought crossed his mind soon after, didn’t she have plenty of powerful colleagues herself? People who would stand by her side in order to help her fend of the might of the current Dizhuang star? The whole thing didn’t make nearly enough sense to him, though looking at her now, it was somewhat clear that he’d know the answer eventually. Afterall, if she had given it no thought at all, she wouldn’t even be bothered about being a star, nor eager to help him with fights, training, or anything else he deemed loosely related to their goal.

..The backstreets were much colder than the main streets of Hongyuan, darker too. An intimidating silence loomed over the area, though it may have just been his pondering making him more anxious. Yolisa didn’ t seem to mind, slinging her sword around, as if she was used to the scenery. If anything, she seemed more focused here than she did in the nest. There wasn’t a word spoken between the two for quite a while, just a mutual understanding that they had a mission, and that the backstreets were nowhere to be messing about.. Atleast thats what Yang understood of it.

The two searched for a good while, not finding a single person in the area. Though Yang had known this area to be quieter in the past, it were never this silent. Neither he or Yolisa could hear any signs of life, other than what they assumed were the faint murmurs of an increasingly far nest.

Thats when Yolisa noticed something on the floor.

Yolisa: “Oh, ooh..! Lad. look at this.”

Yolisa squatted down onto the floor, before standing back up. She must’ve spotted the dead animal slightly further back, but now she was holding what seemed to be a mangled mouse. Its bones were torn out of a rupture in its stomach.

Yang Dingliu: “Eugh.. Put that down. Didn’t I tell you about your cleanliness? D-doing stuff like that doesn’t paint you in a good light, you know? Especially next to me-”

Yolisa: “Fucking hell, chill out lad. It's a dead rat. Bloomin eck’.. D' you not wonder what ad’ that done to it? ..Looks proper purposeful, like someone was harvesting it or some bollocks.”

Despite being repulsed by the carcass, he leaned in to take a closer look. He had seen worse afterall, he figured.

Yang Dingliu: “That's strange.. They didn’t take the meat.”

Yolisa: “What? You were reckonin’ some poor bastard was starving to death and had to scran a rat? That's depressing..”

Yang Dingliu: “..Yeah.. Seems they only took the bone.. I can’t imagine any reason you’d want only the bones of an animal like that.”

Yolisa: “People are fucking strange though aren’t they.. No one's got good reasons to be doing half the shite they do nowadays.”

Yang Dingliu: “Mm.. I feel you on that one, usually people like that end up spewing so much nonsense at me I just stop trying to make sense to them.”

Yolisa: “..Eh.”

Yolisa flicks the rodent's corpse away, wiping her hand on her sword before continuing her search.

Yolisa: “Y’should humour them a bit more sometimes. People usually get sick of you pretty quick when you carry on like that. …But I do agree. It makes me livid too.”

…The silence returned soon after that. There was still no one around. Though a smell, similar to the rotting, copper tasting scent of the rodent's body was slowly becoming more and more pungent. Yang seemed unbothered by the scent. Trying to continue the task as normal, but Yolisa seemed quickly turned off by the smell, pulling her bandages over her nose for a moment.

Yolisa: “Bloody hell..”

Yang Dingliu: “..It might just be from last night, Yolisa.”

He was muttering, being a bit dismissive. His mind still linger on the task, and his question.

Yolisa: “..Yeah, smart one dickhead. They clean up the bodies, I’ve seen it meself. Whatever' s gone of ere’ is either inside one of these buildings, or from today.”

Yang Dingliu: “....You might be right actually, it is unusually quiet..”

Yolisa: “..You reckon there's been some big knobhead whose gone proper livid at some random bullshit and just came and done in a bunch of people for a laugh?”

Yang Dingliu: “Gosh! Maybe it is Yasksha! Man, that’d make it so much easier h-”

…A shuffling could be heard a few buildings away, which turned into a loud almost thumping sound, all without a proper rhythm or reason.

Yolisa: “..Oi mate.”

As the sound grew louder, it became clearer what the noise was. Someone, or perhaps something... Was coming their way.
