As the two walked through the street, looking out for anyone that looked familiar, or at least Yang was. Despite the plan to find the compass, Yolisa had no clue what the “compass” or who the “compass” was or looked like. She looks over to Yang, his eyes wandering through nearby crowds.

Yolisa: “Right.. Where's this shop at Lad? We can’t just sit dossing about the streets all day, right?”

Yang Dingliu: “Eh.. uhm.. Mhm..”

Yang puts his hand to his mouth, looking as if he had just realized he left his wallet at home. Something seemed unfamiliar to him about the whole ordeal, he looked up at the walls of the area, then back to Yolisa, then to a different wall. He knew exactly what had happened by this point, and was trying to figure out how he was going to tell her without embarrassing himself.

Yang Dingliu: “.....”

Yolisa: “..Is something up?”

The two of them stop at a wall. Yang messed with his hands more and more skittishly as he looked around, making a noise only comparable to a creaky door as he turned to his confused friend.

Yang Dingliu: “Sooooo… Ahem.. mm. Mmhm. I’m very sorry, my friend..”

…Yolisa tilted her head. Despite her face being obscured by the bandages on her face, Yang could tell she was trying to figure out what had happened.

It always came as strange to him. Yolisa, despite her expressive speech, always seemed to be unreadable to the people who came to the tavern. There had been many times within the months she had been living with him where she would get into bother with customers due to her seeming “disingenuous”. Maybe it was the intensive training the two had done together, or the shared goal of revenge that had brought them to the same position in the first place, but Yang always felt he could read her expression quite well, perhaps better than anyone he’s ever known before.

Yang Dingliu: “Yolisa, my humble friend. My “humble colleague”. My-”

Yolisa: “What have you done..”

Yolisa’s tone shifted into bother. Trying to figure out what had happened herself. Could he have given her a faulty sword? Perhaps spilt a drink on his clothes the night prior? Just what have this lad could’ve-

..She looked over to the street they had walked down on the way there.

…

Yolisa: “..Yesterday was restructuring day.”

Yang Dingliu: “YESTERDAY WAS REStructuring dayyyyyyy….”

Yang’s words slurred into a grumble as he held his head and slumped over next to a wall, undoubtedly annoyed with himself. He started cursing himself in H corps local tongue. Yolisa huddled herself over, leaning on a wall next to Yang.

Yolisa: “..Mate.”

Yang Dingliu: “What…”

Yolisa: “...What's it with you and never plannin’ ahead like..”

Yang Dingliu: “I wish I knew, Yolisa. Okay? I wish I knew.”

Now looking up at Yolisa, he continued to grumble and pout. She put her head down as if a new weight had been added to her shoulders.

Yolisa: “Bloody hell..”

She would pull out her sword, twirling the handle around in her hand as she waited for one of the two to figure out what to do next. His embarrassment becoming so palpable Yolisa could almost smell it in the air.

The sound of people passing by filled the silence, palanquins and shouting being heard from afar. Neither Yolisa or Yang seemed particularly interested in what the passersby were up to, the street had filled up more since a few moments ago.

Yolisa looked up before she had more chances to sulk or berate Yang, looking at the passersby. Nothing particularly interesting stuck out to her, but it did make her realize something about Yang that she had never picked up on before.

Yolisa: “Oi.”

Yang Dingliu: “What…”

Yolisa: “Where do you get your clothes from anyway?”

Seemingly taken aback by the randomness of the question, Yang looked up at Yolisa, perplexed.

Yang Dingliu: “...What?”

Yolisa: “Fuckin hell, d’you turn deaf or something? WHERE-DO-YOU-GET-YOUR-CLOTHES?”

Yang Dingliu: “T-they’re from a clothing firm in M corp! The clothing lines in M corp aren’t anything like the ones in H corp.. I think that's why I like it- I always had to wear.. "Strange".. clothes from H corp while under Yaksha.. So I take my own clothes in stride- and you seem to like wearing them too, so I–”

Yolisa: “..M corp.. That sounds sick. Sort of. Anyways lad, I only asked you to knock you out of your sulking. I’ve got a plan.”

Yang Dingliu: “Wh- No you didn’t! What’re you some sort of master-”

Yolisa: “Blimming heck it doesn’t matter lad.. Look. Lets quit yapping and get to looking. If we sit and ask people about this "compass", i’m sure we’ll find it, yeah? I can only imagine if it really DOES do what you said there's no way people don’t know about it.”

Yang Dingliu: “..You do know that I said "It’s" a p-”

Yolisa: “Whatever! Whatever.. Lets just get to asking..”

Yang Dingliu: “...”

Something wasn’t sitting right with Yang. He felt a little guilty of not being so thorough with what he told Yolisa, and equally lazy with planning. Surely it didn’t matter that much? She’ll find out soon eventually..?

Yang sighed, and followed Yolisa into the street to ask around.
