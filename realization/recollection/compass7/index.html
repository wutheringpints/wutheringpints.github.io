..As he was about to start questioning what or who could be at the bottom of the dark passage in front of them, any room for uncertainty was escorted out of the area. Yang watched as the creators of the noise passed by the alley, their appearances unmistakable.

Yang Dingliu: “Ring students..? Out here..?”

Yolisa shifted her head to the side to watch with Yang. Fauvist students, passing through the backstreets. Though Yolisa had never seen the movement herself, Yang was able to identify them quickly. Was this the reason that mice had been desecrated before? Had these students searched the entire area for resources?.

It seemed so. Not a single one looked down the passage. Whatever they had been doing had finished its course, and now they were heading back to somewhere unknown to the two.

Yolisa: “..Ring students? You mean like..Those knobheads who walk around with those big fuckoff hammers?”

Yang Dingliu: “..Hammers?”

Yolisa: “The Ring geezer I knew had a hammer.”

Yang Dingliu: “..I don’t think these guys are in the same movement. They’re most certainly aligned with Fauvism.”

Yolisa: “..Dunno what that is.”

Yang Dingliu: “They make art with animal parts, basically.”

Yolisa: “Oooooh.. So that's why they’re here..”

Yolisa: “They must’ve figured they could get a piece of heishou, eh? Whacked up some poor bastard with a bolus and stole their skin, Hm?”

Yang was taken aback a bit, he never thought about that part. It explained why the backstreets were so quiet.. She could be right there.

Yang Dingliu: “Maybe, actually..”

Yang Dingliu: “Doesn’t matter! We need to stay back here until they’re gone.”

Yolisa: “Whys that? I thought the students were like the lowest of the low and that. We could definitely take them.”

Yang Dingliu: “Sure, but with that many around, it might mean theres a higher member roaming around. Like a docent or a maestro.. I doubt a maestro would be here, but perhaps a docent..?”

Yolisa: “...Right. I better get to fight someone sometime soon lad. All that trainings gonna be for nish if not, y’know?”

Yang Dingliu: “I do know..! We just have to stay quiet until they pass by. Neither of us want to end up as some shitty art installation.”

Yolisa: “Oi. I’ll ave’ you know if they’re anything like my old mate, their art’ll be pretty top notch, yeah?”

Yang Dingliu: “..Mm. Yeah.”

Yang had absolutely no idea who her "old mate" was. She would tell stories like this all the time, they pretty much all blend together. Something was different about this time though, the defence that she’d come to for the people she knew sounded more positive this time, like she was fond of whoever her friend was.

They continued to wait in the cold. Watching the Fauvist group grow fewer and fewer as they passed by, until the chatter felt far away again. Yolisa was the first one to step out of the shadows.

Yolisa: “Whew.. Wonder where they’re off to then.”

Yang Dingliu: “..It’s none of our concern, to be honest. What we need to do is find what we've been looking for.”

..Yolisa put her hand to her chin, pondering.

Yolisa: “..Ang’ on a minute.”

Yang Dingliu: “..Yeah?”

Yolisa: “If they’ve just been looking for people to bust up..-”

Yang Dingliu: “..-Yeeeeah?”

Yolisa: “And we’re looking for this compass down ere’?..-”

Yang Dingliu: “...Yeaaah-”

Yang Dingliu: “...”

..Yangs face shifted into one of horror, as if Yolisa had pointed out that his old mentor was standing right behind him this whole time.

Yang Dingliu: “...They might’ve have found-..”

Yolisa: “Mm. mm.”

Yang Dingliu: “..We need to go.”

Yolisa: “-Yes we do lad.”

And they did exactly that. Rushing down the same way the Fauvist groups had been marching just moments prior.
