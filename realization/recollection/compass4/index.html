After walking for a while, Yang and Yolisa stop in front of a small shop.

Yolisa: “..? Is this the place? Did we find it?”

Yang Dingliu: “No, not yet.. Just a normal store. Just- wait outside.. Im gonna go grab a drink.”

Yolisa: “Bugger.. You ought to stop getting me excited for shite that you know we’re looking for Lad.”

Yang Dingliu: “I'm sorry, alright? Jeez.. Not even allowed to be thirsty now.. I'm going to get a drink.. You want one?”

Yolisa: “Nah, I’ll stay on the lookout though, yeah?”

Yang Dingliu: “Fine by me~”

Trying to ease the mood a bit after his major blunder, Yang spoke those last words in an almost comedically smooth tone with how over the top he was being. Yolisa watched him disappear into the shop as she found a nearby wall to sit by, putting her sword behind the brick.

Yolisa: “...What a strange lad he is.”

Yolisa continued to mutter to herself as she watched the crowd walk pass by, trying to get a read on anyone she saw as “interesting”. After a while, Yang walked back over to her, holding some sort of carton.

Yolisa: “-What’d you get then?”

Yang Dingliu: “Strawberry iced tea.”

Yolisa: “...I used to have a weird obsession with strawberries, I’d stick them in literally everything I made at the bar. Did I tell you that?”

Yang Dingliu: “Huh. I think i’d get bored of drinking strawberries all day..”

Yolisa: “...It’s whatever I ‘spose.. I’ll have to go back there at some point soon, no? They’re probably worried about me and all that carry on.”

Yang Dingliu: “...”

He would shoot a suspicious look at Yolisa while she looked away, unsure of something.

Yang Dingliu: “...Are you sure?”

Yolisa: “..? What’re you on about?”

Yang Dingliu: “..Yolisa… Don’t you think that maybe…-”

Yolisa: “-Ooh, look. They look interesting.. Oi! C’mere you.”

Yolisa would sit up a bit, trying to keep a friendly tone with the passerby… Yang decided it was best to stop his questioning for the time being, quelling his concern.

Yolisa: “Eya mate. You reckon you’ve seen a "compass" shop recently? After yesterday anyway.”

Passerby: “...No ma’am.. Sorry. Im in a bit of a rush so–.”

Yolisa leaned back a bit, pretending to reach back for the sword.

Yolisa: “Buuuugger.. Thats a shame lad.”

Passerby: “! M-ma’am.. I’m being honest! Theres nothing of the sort around here.. Unless you’re thinking of the journalism firm?”

Yolisa: “...”

Yolisa looks over to Yang, who at this point is making a loud slurping noise from his cup while trying to look unbothered. He cocks his eyebrow, before nodding, Yolisa taking this as a signal to retreat from her sword.

Yolisa: “..Go on.”

Passerby: “The journalism firm that was mingling with the seven associations a while ago. I think it was around the hierarchy evaluation..? I don’t know where they are now.. Sorry.”

Yolisa: “..Blimming eck’ Yang you didn’t tell me we were finding some bloody newspaper agent. Maybe you would have found them in that shop back there. Go on, go check the paper aisle.”

Yang Dingliu: “Shut up you-! You don’t even know if that's the proper place to be looking!”

Yolisa: “Yeah no.. You’re right, you’re right.. We can go have a look for journalists though. Might point us in a better direction. Thanks La–”

The passerby had left already. Seems they really were in a rush.

Yolisa: “..Dickhead.”

Yang Dingliu: “..Well, I guess we’re looking for local journalists then.”

Yolisa: “I know lad..”

Yang Dingliu: “..Where would someone like that even hang around..? If I know the one we’re looking for well, then-.. …hm.”

Yolisa: “...Backstreets?”

Yang Dingliu: “Backstreets..?”

Yolisa: “Reckon there's some pretty minted stories back there, no? With all the people that get done out back. We should go look.”

Yang Dingliu: “Thats a… Good idea, actually.”

Yolisa: “See? I’m well sick at this Lad. We'll bang this out in no time.”

Yang Dingliu: “Yeah..! Lets get going then, …Mate!”

Yolisa picked up her sword, walking towards Yang as he started walking in the general direction of where the backstreets usually are.

Yolisa: “..Don’t call me mate by the way. Not- cause we’re not mates. It just sounds proper stupid when you say it.”
